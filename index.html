Mission

As a higher education institution, PNC is dedicated to equipping students with the knowledge, skills, and values they need to reach their professional goals, while preparing them to lead and serve toward national development.

Vision

PNC aspires to be a premier institution of higher learning in Region IV-A, producing globally competitive and values-driven professionals and leaders who contribute to community development and nation-building.
